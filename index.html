01 · Who We Are

Security Engineering
& Research

We are seven cybersecurity professionals who built, broke, and defended systems together — across SOC operations, detection engineering, cloud security, GRC, offensive security research, and enterprise security leadership. Between us, we hold BTech degrees in Cybersecurity and Computer Science, graduating in 2025 and 2026.

What began as a shared drive to go deeper than coursework — through independent labs, hackathons, CTFs, and personal builds — evolved into something more deliberate: a team that architects production-grade security infrastructure, conducts real adversarial simulations, and ships work that stands up under scrutiny.

We have collectively held positions at Gardiyan System Security Technologies (Ankara, Turkey), Quantix IT Ltd (Coventry, England), CyberSRC Consultancy (Noida, India), and Cisco SOC Global (across East Africa, Europe, and Asia). We have built zero trust platforms, automated SOC workflows, authored high-fidelity detection rules, executed penetration tests, and won blockchain security bounties. The work speaks first.

SOC & Detection Engineering
Alert triage, SIEM rule authoring, SOAR automation, incident response playbooks — across Sentinel, Sumo Logic, Wazuh, Splunk, and ELK Stack.
Cloud & Zero Trust Security
Multi-cloud security monitoring across AWS, Azure, and GCP — IAM, CSPM, Kubernetes hardening, and Zero Trust architecture with Keycloak and Pomerium.
Adversarial Simulation & Research
Red team operations, MITRE ATT&CK-aligned simulations, penetration testing, threat intelligence programmes, and post-quantum cryptography research.
GRC & Compliance
Risk assessments, audit readiness, policy development, and compliance frameworks — ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, CIS Benchmarks, and GDPR alignment.
Enterprise Security Leadership
Security operations leadership spanning three continents — East Africa, Europe, and Asia — with enterprise-scale SOC management and detection engineering across Cisco global infrastructure.
